About these notes

Markets & Policy follows the last complete Beijing calendar day and separates new evidence from historical context. Reading Economics selects scholarly work daily, records questions, methods, findings and caveats, and keeps foundational reading accessible in a separate library. Research interpretations are provisional and linked to the underlying sources.

Daily review windows follow the Beijing calendar day (China Standard Time, UTC+8). Academic paper publication dates and selection dates are stated separately.

For research and informational purposes only. Not investment advice. Information is sourced from publicly available materials and may be revised as new evidence emerges.
